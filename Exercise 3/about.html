About the Data

Data source

The television dataset used in the Data Story (see the Televisions page) is drawn from the Australian Government's Energy Rating registration database, the public register of Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards (GEMS)-regulated appliances approved for sale in Australia. The snapshot used here contains 4,724 currently approved TV model registrations (extracted Feb 2026), including brand, model, screen size, panel technology, standby/average power draw, and labelled energy consumption (kWh/year).

Data processing

Screen size was converted from centimetres to inches to match how TVs are marketed and compared in Australian retail. Models were grouped into six size bands that reflect common retail categories (32" and under, 40–43", 50–55", 60–65", 70–75", 80"+). Annual running costs were estimated by multiplying each model's labelled kWh/year figure by an assumed electricity rate of AU$0.30/kWh, a rough mid-range figure — actual household tariffs vary by state, retailer, and plan. We noticed brand names are recorded inconsistently in the raw register (e.g. "SAMSUNG", "Samsung", and "SAMSUNG ELECTRONICS" all appear as separate values) — this wasn't corrected for the charts shown here since our story groups by size and technology, not brand, but it would need cleaning before any brand-level comparison.

Privacy

This dataset contains only product registration information (brand, model, technical specifications) submitted by manufacturers/importers to a government regulator. It contains no personal or household-level data, and no individuals are identifiable from it.

Accuracy and limitations

  • Labelled energy consumption figures come from standardised lab testing (AS/NZS 62087.1:2010) under a fixed test pattern and viewing duration, not real households — actual usage will vary with brightness settings, viewing hours, and picture mode.
  • The $0.30/kWh rate is an approximation for illustration, not a bill forecast; real electricity prices vary significantly across Australian states and retailers.
  • The 80"+ "LCD" (non-LED) technology group has a very small sample in this dataset (n = 18) compared to LCD (LED) (n = 535) and OLED (n = 103), so that particular average should be read cautiously.
  • "Country" in the raw data refers to country of manufacture, not country of sale, and was not used in this story.

Ethics

The data story intentionally reports on aggregated size and technology categories rather than naming or ranking individual brands, to avoid unfairly singling out manufacturers based on a dataset with known recording inconsistencies. All cost estimates and assumptions (electricity rate, size bands) are stated explicitly so readers can judge how much weight to put on the exact dollar figures rather than treating them as precise predictions.
