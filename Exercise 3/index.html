Appliance Energy Consumption in Australia

Household appliances make up a significant share of residential electricity use across Australia. Understanding how much energy common appliances consume can help households make informed decisions about usage habits, upgrades, and overall electricity costs.

Data Story: For Australian households shopping for a new TV

Does a bigger TV cost a lot more to run?

Using the Australian Government's public Energy Rating registration database (4,724 currently approved TV models), we looked at how much screen size and panel technology actually affect a TV's running cost — the part of TV shopping that's easy to overlook next to the sticker price. Short answer: yes, dramatically — and if you want a very large screen, the technology you choose can still save you real money each year.
